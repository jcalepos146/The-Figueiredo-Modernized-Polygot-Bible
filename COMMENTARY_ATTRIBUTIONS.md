## Method

Each formerly blank commentary source was checked against the OCR text or scan of the corresponding volume of António Pereira de Figueiredo’s New Testament. The attribution printed at the close of the note was retained. Where Pereira presents a synthesis and signs the note, the source is listed as **Pereira**. Where the note explicitly rests on several authorities without a single closing author, those authorities are listed together.

The labels **Traditional commentary** and **Traditional Catholic commentary** elsewhere in the site identify modern editorial syntheses, not anonymous quotations from the Figueiredo scans. They were therefore not reassigned to historical writers without evidence.
